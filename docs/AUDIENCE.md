# Who code-to-3d is for

The through-line: every persona has taste and intent but no modeling hours —
except the pros, who have hours but hate the boring 80%. Two rings.

## Outer ring — can't (or won't) model. Funds the build.

| Persona | Job-to-be-done | Brings | Refuses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Solo builder | promo + explainer 3D, no hiring | taste, an agent subscription | learning Blender |
| Agent-native dev | product visuals, docs diagrams, social clips | JS/Python to tweak, CI | hand-placed vertices |
| Scientist/educator | accurate figures (labels, ortho views) | domain truth, references | Illustrator + screenshots |
| Content maker | fast social 3D, memes | trends, timing, audacity | render farms, fees, waiting |

## Inner ring — models daily. Sets the quality bar.

| Persona | Grunt the agent eats | Gate they keep |
|---|---|---|
| Architect | drawing sets, orthos, turntables, revisions | massing, light, material truth |
| Interior designer | variant churn, mood-board → scene | palette, feel, composition |
| Product designer | CMF variants, exploded views, callouts | form, surfacing decisions |
| CAD engineer | drawing extraction, BOM views, conversion | tolerances, engineering truth |
| VFX / CGI artist | previs, layout, lookdev variants | final pixels, comp |

Pros don't get replaced — they get plugged at the file-format border
(FBX, glTF, USD). Win one trade first: architects (drawing sets = our ortho
+ labels strength) or product designers (exploded views = our best trick).

## Their stack (meet them there)

Agents: Claude Code / Codex / Cursor / OpenCode / Gemini.
Metal: Blender (grudgingly), Three.js, Figma, gh, npm, CI.

## Name note

code-to-3d: 7/10. Rides the code-to-video parallel, says what it does.
Costs one clarifying sentence per newcomer ("scenes, not blobs" — not a
text-to-3D mesh generator). Worth it for the category claim.
